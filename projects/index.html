LandHawk

2026

A parcel recon console for Florida land. Fly around a dark map, click any parcel, and the dock fills in — ownership, acreage, deed history, flood, soils, elevation — streamed per source as each one answers. Deterministic data all the way down: no LLM in the loop.

YTranscript

2026

A browser extension that pulls the transcript out of any YouTube video — timestamps, chapters, pause-inferred paragraphs — as plain text or Obsidian-ready Markdown. Point it at a channel or playlist and it archives the whole thing to disk, one file per video.

Download v1.1.1 (.zip) — unzip, then “Load unpacked” at chrome://extensions

Amex Offers — Add All

2026

Loops through the American Express offers page and adds every offer to your card — re-querying after each click, scrolling past the lazy-loading, until none remain. Replaces the console snippet that quietly stopped matching anything.

Download v1.0.0 (.zip) — unzip, then “Load unpacked” at chrome://extensions

Penny Tracker

2026

Home Depot quietly marks dead inventory down to $0.01, but only at the register — the pricing API never admits it. So this tracked the tell instead, pulling per-store stock and aisle / bay for candidate SKUs and rendering a scannable UPC‑A barcode to check at the shelf. Archived snapshot: the data is frozen at its last run, the filters and barcodes still work.
